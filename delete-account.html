Account Deletion Request

Soru – Food Delivery allows registered users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

If you no longer want to use your Soru account, you can request account deletion by following the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address you use to communicate with Soru support.
  2. Include the mobile number registered with your Soru account.
  3. Use the subject: Soru Account Deletion Request
  4. Our team will review the request and process the account deletion.
Request Account Deletion
Important: Please provide the mobile number associated with your Soru account so that we can identify the account and process your request.

Data That May Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, applicable personal information associated with your account may be deleted, including:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Account and authentication information
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Location information associated with the account
  • Cart information
  • Customer profile information
  • Reviews and ratings, where applicable

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required by applicable law, accounting requirements, security purposes, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate business requirements.

Examples may include certain order, transaction, payment, security, or audit records.

Where information is required to be retained, it will be kept only for the applicable retention period and handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Please note: Account deletion may be permanent and cannot be reversed once the deletion process has been completed.

Account Deletion from the App

If the account deletion option is available in the Soru application, you can also request deletion directly from:

Profile → Settings → Delete Account

Follow the confirmation steps displayed in the application to submit your deletion request.
