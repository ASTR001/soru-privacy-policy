About Soru

Connecting customers, delivery partners, and businesses through a simple, reliable, and modern food delivery experience.

What is Soru?

Soru is a modern food delivery platform designed to make ordering, delivering, and managing food services simple, fast, and convenient.

Soru brings customers, delivery partners, and businesses together through an integrated digital ecosystem. From placing an order to delivering it to the customer's doorstep, Soru helps simplify the entire process.

Soru Driver

The Soru Driver app helps delivery partners efficiently manage assigned orders and complete deliveries.
